USB flasherThe People's RNode

Write the OS image to a Bobcat G290/G295's internal storage from this page, over the board's OTG USB port. No SD card, no tools to install. Experimental: the steps are tested against an emulated board and the same protocol on real hardware by hand, but this page has not yet flashed a unit.

This erases the unit's internal storage. Use the bobcat-g295-emmc.img.xz image from the releases, not the standalone SD image (it only boots from an SD card).

1. The image

Choose bobcat-g295-emmc.img.xz (or the unpacked .img). It is read in your browser and never uploaded.

2. Settings (optional)

The text of bobcat-install.conf. It goes into the boot partition, so the first boot joins Wi-Fi and applies it. Leave it empty to install with defaults (needs a wired network).

Open the generator

3. Put the board in download mode

  1. Remove the SD card. Connect the board's OTG USB port to this computer.
  2. Hold Recovery, press and release Reset, then release Recovery after about two seconds.
  3. Press Connect and choose USB download gadget (2207:350a).

4. Flash

The board restarts into fastboot (18d1:4d00). Press Connect and choose it. The flash takes a few minutes; keep the cable in and this tab open.

Browser and driver requirements
  • Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser (WebUSB). Firefox and Safari do not support it.
  • Linux: the browser needs permission to open the device. For example in /etc/udev/rules.d/99-bobcat.rules: 
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTR{idVendor}=="2207", MODE="0666"
SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTR{idVendor}=="18d1", ATTR{idProduct}=="4d00", MODE="0666"
  • Windows: the two USB devices need the WinUSB driver (for example with Zadig), or the page cannot open them.
  • macOS: usually works without anything extra.
